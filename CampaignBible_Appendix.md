## E) Glossary
- **Crown Tax:** annual payment owed to the king.
- **Demesne:** the count’s personal lands worked for his benefit.
- **Levy:** temporary manpower raised from the populace.
- **Order:** abstract stability rating (0–6).
- **Render:** payment in produce/coin owed by villagers.
