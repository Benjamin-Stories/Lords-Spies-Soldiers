## Session 2 — Harrowgate: First Day, Household, and First Steps (2026-01-28)
**Date:** Day 1, 26th of Frostwane (late winter)

### Arrival and first impressions
- The campaign opened **on a ridge** overlooking **Eastbridge** and **Castle Harrowgate**, per player preference (seeing the castle first from afar).
- Harrowgate is described as an **older but lived-in noble seat**: great hall, courtyard, keep with apartments; one tower is **half-ruined** and some wallwork is patched.
- At the gate, Ben presented the **royal charter**; Holt verified it and admitted Ben and Tomas.

### Canon clarification: Harrowgate is running on a skeleton crew
Player requested realism: after the old count’s execution, there should not be a bustling paid staff.
Current Harrowgate staffing (bare minimum):
- **Garrick Holt** (“Old Holt”) — caretaker-castellan/steward-by-necessity; keeps the keys; lives in-castle.
- **Guards (4):** **Hask, Renn, Coll, Weller**.
- **Stable lad:** **Jory**.
- **Housekeeper/cook:** **Mistress Anwen**, with her teenage daughter **Mira**.
- **Yard-hand/repairs:** **Old Bram**.
- Holt stated **wages have not been paid properly for some time**; order maintained by rationing and promises.

### Tour and introductions
- Ben and Tomas took a **tour** led by Holt: great hall, kitchens/pantry, stores/granary, cellar/wood store, armory, gatehouse/wallwalk.
- **Supplies observed:** modest, carefully rationed food stores and firewood; enough to function but not comfortable.
- The armory contains **a few serviceable spears**, **battered shields**, and **repaired mail**.
- Ben gathered the entire remaining household in the **great hall**, thanked them for keeping Harrowgate alive, and invited introductions.
- Household needs voiced:
  - Anwen: **salt and lamp oil**, more hands if coin allows.
  - Jory: **hay low**, tack wearing.
  - Bram: **tools thin**.
  - Hask: four guards is **too few**; concern about **thaw-season trouble** and **Eastbridge**.

### Pacing rule reaffirmed (table agreement)
- Player requested **slower, more natural pacing**: no skipping ahead past chosen beats; early sessions focus on **discovery and relationships**, not forced management decisions.

### Lunch conversations and plans
- Ben and Tomas ate privately; both noted Harrowgate feels **heavy** and **tired**, but not broken.
- Tentative intent for tomorrow: **ride to Eastbridge** first, then villages later (not staged, low-pressure observation).

### Holt’s briefing: Eastbridge and county basics
- Eastbridge governance described in local terms:
  - **Town Speaker** (not “mayor”) as the public voice.
  - **Master of the River Tolls** (bridge tolls).
  - **Market wardens**.
  - **Shrine-keeper** (social influence).
- Peacekeeping: a **rotating town watch/militia** of householders with cudgels/old spears; bridge chokepoint helps deter trouble.
- Tolls and taxes:
  - Bridge tolls are the **cleanest coin**; in the count’s absence the town’s men have been collecting/holding them to keep things running.
  - Other revenues: village dues (mostly in kind/labor), market fees, and seasonal river trade takes.
- Land: Holt outlined **demesne**, **tenant holdings**, and **commons**; exact demesne measures require estate rolls (status unknown).

### Steward search begins (both lanes)
- Ben directed Holt to seek a competent ledger-trained administrator: **competence first**, local if possible, outside if needed.
- Decision: open **both lanes**—local inquiries in Eastbridge and an external search.
- Holt drafted a letter to **Asterhall** under Ben’s name/authority requesting an **estate clerk** (with potential advancement), to travel at once.
- Canon note: Ben possesses a **seal/signet** sufficient to seal official letters.

### End state / current scene
- Ben spent the remaining daylight **talking with Holt** and walking the castle with Tomas, then went to bed.
- Next morning begins with Ben **waking and looking out the window**; Holt is outside the door noting the **Asterhall rider** can depart within the hour.

## Session 1 — Opener Scene (immediate situation)
**Date:** Day 1, 26th of Frostwane (late winter)

### Immediate situation
Ben Paran and Tomas Rell crest the last ridge and see **Castle Harrowgate** above the river road. Below, the market town **Eastbridge** sits near the stone bridge—smoke rises thin, and the roads look churned into mud.

At Harrowgate’s gatehouse a tired guard watches your approach. Inside the yard stands a grey-bearded caretaker in a worn surcoat with a ring of keys: **Garrick Holt** (called “Old Holt”). Two townsmen linger near the outer yard, pretending not to listen.

Holt’s eyes go to your seal case.

> “Name and business.”

### Player options (choose 1; you can also mix)
A) **Assert authority:** announce yourself as Count Ben Paran and demand entry under the charter; request immediate muster and keys.

B) **Build rapport:** ask for Holt by name, keep it calm; request a private word and a short tour before any public display.

C) **Claim the town first:** ride past to **Eastbridge** to be seen; have the charter read in the market square and measure public mood.

D) **Information first:** have Tomas quietly watch routines (guards, supplies, who whispers to whom) while you speak; ask Holt who sent the townsmen.

**End-of-day rule reminder:** The day will not advance until you say you go to bed.
