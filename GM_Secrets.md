# GM SECRETS (Do Not Show Players)

## Design goals
- Keep Ben ignorant at start; reveal truth through audits, witnesses, documents, and consequences.
- Present dilemmas where every option has a cost (Order vs Silver vs legitimacy).

## Hidden truths (choose/confirm during play as clues accumulate)
### The executed count’s “treason” (truth matrix)
Pick one as the real truth; let rumors point to several.
1) **Guilty:** he sold access to Eastbridge bridge/toll rights to a rival house.
2) **Framed:** a court faction needed his lands and used Ben’s elevation as cover.
3) **Embezzlement labeled treason:** he withheld Crown Tax; execution was a warning.
4) **Succession scheme:** he backed a quiet claimant; the crown crushed it.

**Recommended for long campaign:** #2 (Framed) OR #3 (Embezzlement→treason). Both generate court intrigue + local grudges.

## Immediate hidden pressures in Graymere (fronts)
1) **Ledger rot:** missing entries, double-counted stores, falsified renders. Someone benefits.
2) **Key-person network:** Holt may be honest but compromised (family hostage, debts, old oath).
3) **Town oligarchy:** Eastbridge’s leading merchants/carters quietly ran things during neglect.
4) **Village power:** one village headman is a “king” in practice; will resist reeves.

## Early twists (deploy 1–2 within first 7 in-game days)
- The strongroom opens: coin is **not** where it should be; a sealed chest bears a different signet.
- A messenger arrives with a polite but pointed note: the Crown expects the **midsummer payment** without excuse.
- A dead man is found near the bridge with a toll token in his mouth (message killing).
- Tomas recognizes a guard as former palace staff—why is he here?

## Mid arcs (seasonal)
### Arc A: “The Audit War” (late winter → spring)
- Ben tries to establish administration: hire scribe/steward/reeves.
- Opposition: town council stalls; villages hide grain; someone sabotages patrols.
- Success condition: reliable revenue baseline + Order ≥ 4.

### Arc B: “Bridge and Blood” (spring → autumn)
- Eastbridge tolls become central. Competing claims surface.
- A neighboring lord pressures for “shared jurisdiction.”
- A trade dispute risks famine pricing.

### Arc C: “Court Hooks” (anytime)
- Court faction offers help (loan, men, marriage prospect) with strings.
- The princess’s gratitude can be leveraged—but creates enemies.

## Long arcs (years)
- A rival house attempts to absorb Graymere via marriage/wardship/debt.
- A royal reform of taxation threatens noble autonomy; Ben must pick sides.
- A border conflict turns “prosperous peace” into mobilization; Ben’s readiness matters.

## GM operating notes
- Keep numbers uncertain until Ben invests in **records + trustworthy staff**.
- Make winter scarcity real: stores are power.
- Reward competence: stable Order increases realized revenue and reduces bandit events.

## Seed list (dropable hooks)
- “A mill that doesn’t grind because the wheel was sold.”
- “A village where the dead are buried with toll tokens.”
- “A widow of the executed count petitions for a dowry chest.”
- “A royal inspector arrives early—unannounced.”
