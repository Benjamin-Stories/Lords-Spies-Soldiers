# Graymere: A County Won in Blood (No-Magic Medieval Domain Campaign)

## Quick pitch
You are **Ben Paran**, a young, formerly landless knight of the royal guard. After saving **Princess Maeryn Cauldrin** from an assassination attempt, the king granted you the title **Count of Graymere**—and the lands and castle of an executed “traitor” count.

You arrive at **Castle Harrowgate** at the **end of winter** with only your horse, your seal, and your childhood friend **Tomas Rell**.

The campaign is about:
- **Politics & intrigue** (court factions, local power, rival nobles)
- **War & coercion** (levies, raids, fortifications, lethal fights)
- **Ruling the fief** (taxes, stores, labor, justice, projects)

There is **no magic** and **no Christian church**. Religion exists as local shrines and customs, but nobles and law drive power.

## How to run/play
### Core play loop
1. **Scene play** (talk, investigate, travel, judge disputes, fight when forced).
2. **Decide rulings** (justice, appointments, spending, projects).
3. **Track the domain state** (money, stores, labor, order).
4. **End the day only when the player says “I go to bed.”** Then the game advances.

### Time rules
- **Time advances only when you explicitly go to bed.**
- **Weekly tick**: every 7 nights slept, apply routine costs and minor developments.
- **Seasonal tick**: every 90 nights slept, apply taxes/harvest/major project progress.

### Domain management (medium complexity)
Track:
- **Silver** (coin)
- **Stores** (food supply)
- **Labor** (workforce availability; levies reduce it)
- **Order** (0–6; affects compliance, banditry, revenue)

Expect to begin with **unknown** numbers and build certainty through audits, appointments, and inspections.

### Realism constraints (important)
- Counts do **not** field huge standing armies. You may keep a **small garrison/household men**, but most manpower is **levies** (costly in labor and order).
- The Crown expects a **Crown Tax** and cares about stability.
